  • Americano, hot: espresso → 8–12 oz hot water
  • Americano, iced: quad shot → ice → filtered water

  • Cortado: espresso + 4 oz silky steamed milk

  • Mocha: espresso + 1 oz chocolate, stir, add milk

6 · Signature & specialty drinks

Pistachio cardamom latte

  • Hot: 9 oz milk + 1 pump each syrup
  • Iced: 10 oz milk + 1 pump each → ice → espresso
  • Garnish: pistachio powder

Desert Rose / Midnight Rose

  • 2 pumps rose syrup + 1.5 oz tahini (blond or black)
  • Froth with 6 oz milk
  • Espresso shot on the side
  • Garnish: black seed

Pistachio mocha

  • Espresso over 1 oz chocolate
  • 9 oz milk + 2 pumps pistachio + 1 oz pistachio butter (frothed)
  • Iced: drizzle chocolate and pistachio butter inside the cup first
  • Garnish: pistachio powder

Velvet pistachio

  • Espresso over 1 oz pistachio butter
  • 2–4 oz frothed milk
  • Top with whipped cream + pistachio syrup
  • Garnish: pistachio powder and butter

Espresso tonic

  1. Ice + 1 pump vanilla + ½ oz lemon juice.
  2. Sparkling water → espresso on top.

Turkish coffee

  1. Heat 6 oz water in the ibrik.
  2. Add 2 spoons coffee as the water boils.
  3. Stop when the crema darkens.
Part 2

Using the inventory app

Open inventory.brbcoffee-atx.com on the shop tablet or your phone. No login. Every count and flag you make goes straight to the manager's order list, so keep it honest and current.

When to use it: count during your shift whenever you open a new case or notice something running low, and before you leave. If you used the last one, mark it Out right away.

Type your name

Tap the Counting as box at the top right and type your name. The device remembers it, so you only do this once per phone or tablet.

Your name shows next to every count, so the manager knows who to ask about it.

Count what's on hand

On the Count tab, items are grouped by where we buy them (HEB, Odeko, Medici, Other). Read the note under each group. It tells you things like order cutoffs.

  • Tap + or − to change the number, or tap the number and type it.
  • Count in the unit shown under the item name (bag, gallon, sleeve of 50…).
  • A ? means nobody has counted it yet.

The pills under the search bar (HEB, Medici…) jump straight to that group. The red number is how many items there need attention.

Flag Low or Out

  • Low We still have some, but it needs to be on the next order.
  • Out None left. Tap it the moment you use the last one.

Tap the same button again to clear the flag once the stock is back.

Tap the Low & out filter to see only flagged items. Use it at the end of your shift to double-check the list before handing off.

Ask for something new: "We could use…"

Need something that isn't on the list, have a product idea, or found broken gear? Tap ＋ We could use…, type it, and tap Add.

Check the open requests first so you don't add the same thing twice.

Check the On hand tab

The On hand tab is read-only. It shows the latest count, when it was counted, and a status:

  • In stock, Low, Out, or Not counted
  • On order means the manager already ordered it. Don't buy it yourself.

Search and Source / Category switch work the same on both tabs.

If it says "That change didn't save": check the Wi-Fi and tap again. The page reconnects by itself.

Part 4 · Most important

Cleaning standards

A clean bar is part of every drink we serve. These are the standards every barista is held to, on every shift.

What we expect from you

  1. Clean as you go. Don't save messes for later. Wipe spills, purge the wand, and rinse pitchers as you work. A busy rush is not an excuse to leave the bar dirty.
  2. Nothing is someone else's job. If you see it, clean it. Shift lists split the big tasks, but every barista keeps the space clean.
  3. Leave it better than you found it. The next shift should be able to start making drinks the minute they walk in.
  4. Finish your list before you clock out. Work through your shift's checklist, initial each line, and sign the bottom.
  5. Can't finish something? Say so. Tell the next shift and your manager what's left and why. Write it on the sheet. Never skip a task quietly.
  6. Food safety comes first. Use sanitizer at the right strength, keep raw dairy cold, label and date what you open, and wash your hands after cleaning and before handling food or drinks.
  7. Customers can see the bar. Counters, the baklava display, and the condiment area should look ready at all times.
  8. Report broken or worn gear. Use "We could use…" in the inventory app, and tell your manager if it affects drink quality or safety.

Who does what

Cleaning is split by shift so nothing falls through the cracks. The day / shared shift keeps the bar clean during service and does a mid-day reset before handing off. The closing shift does the full equipment clean and sets up the shop to open clean the next day.

Day / shared shift

During your shift

  • Purge and wipe the steam wand after every drink
  • Flush the group head and wipe the basket between shots
  • Rinse milk pitchers after each use
  • Wipe spills right away and keep counters dry
  • Empty the knock box before it's full
  • Keep cups, lids, plates and napkins stocked
  • Take out trash when full
  • Check the bathroom every 2 hours and log it on Sheet C

Before you leave (hand-off reset)

  • Wipe down the big fridge
  • Wipe down the small fridge
  • Pull and wash the bar mats under the matcha station
  • Pull and wash the syrup mats (washing machine)
  • Pull and wash the sauce mats
  • Wipe sticky syrup pumps and sauce bottles
  • Quick wipe of the baklava display, glass and surfaces
  • Bathroom check: restock and wipe down
  • Mark anything low or out in the inventory app and tell the closer

Closing shift

Restocking

  • Refill the chocolate sauce bottle
  • Refill the pistachio butter
  • Check and refill other low syrups and bases

Espresso machine

  • Backflush group heads with espresso cleaner (5 rounds × 10 s)
  • Purge and wipe down steam wands
  • Brush and clean portafilters and baskets
  • Empty and clean the drip tray
  • Empty and clean the knock box

Grinder

  • Empty the grinder using the slider, then purge the remaining shots

Shop

  • Wipe down counters and high-touch surfaces
  • Full bathroom clean (Sheet C)
  • Sweep and mop floors
  • Take out all trash

Bathroom

Customers judge the whole shop by the bathroom. Check it every 2 hours while we're open, and clean it fully at close.

Quick check (every 2 hours)

  • Restock toilet paper, paper towels, and hand soap
  • Wipe the sink, faucet, and counter
  • Check the toilet seat and bowl; clean if needed
  • Empty the trash if it's half full or more
  • Pick up paper and spot-mop spills on the floor
  • Write the time and your initials on the log (Sheet C)

Full clean (closing)

  • Scrub the inside of the toilet bowl with toilet cleaner and brush
  • Disinfect the seat, lid, base, and flush handle
  • Clean and disinfect the sink, faucet, and counter
  • Clean the mirror
  • Disinfect the door handle, lock, light switch, and dispensers
  • Empty the trash and replace the liner
  • Restock toilet paper, paper towels, and soap
  • Sweep and mop the floor
Keep bathroom tools separate. Bathroom gloves, brushes, cloths, and mop never touch the bar or food areas. Wash your hands after every bathroom check before you go back behind the bar.

Weekly

  • Clean the grinder hopper and burr area
  • Clean the top grill on the espresso machine
  • Descale brewers
  • Deep clean refrigerator shelves
  • Clean the espresso machine top grill
  • Scrub bathroom walls, door, and floor edges

Monthly

  • Check filters and calibration
  • Schedule preventive maintenance

Printable checklists follow: day shift, closing shift, bathroom, and a weekly sign-off log.
